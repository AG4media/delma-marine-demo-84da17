The idea

Most shop websites are a grid. Yours should be a salesperson.

A typical shop website

  • Hundreds of products in a grid
  • Search, filter, sort
  • The customer has to know what they need
  • No idea what fits their boat
  • "Out of stock" and that's the end
  • One item, then they leave

The Delma way

  • Asks what you want to do on the water
  • Shows the boat; tap a part, see what fits there
  • Knows live stock in every branch, and the delivery date if not
  • Suggests the full setup, not one item
  • Closes: books, takes the deposit, follows up
  • Remembers you: your boat, your history, your nearest branch

Segment 2 · Plans, warranty, marina

Keeping customers for the life of the boat.

Maintenance plans

Sold online, at the counter, or with a new engine or boat. Visits scheduled into the right workshop, reminders by date or engine hours, renewal online. Fleet customers (government, fishermen): all boats in one place, one monthly invoice.

Warranty claims

Mercury and other brand claims tracked from the job: claimed, approved, paid. Warranty dates per serial number; a job on a unit under warranty opens the claim automatically.

Mangrove Marine

Berths and dry storage on a map, contracts per boat and owner, monthly billing with reminders, owners' requests in a daily list, extra charges onto the owner's account. The marina boat is the same record the workshop services.
