18
Segment 2 · Plans, warranty, marina

Keeping customers for the life of the boat.

Maintenance plans

Sold online, at the counter, or with a new engine or boat. Visits scheduled into the right workshop, reminders by date or engine hours, renewal online. Fleet customers: all boats in one place, one monthly invoice.

Warranty claims

Mercury and other brand claims tracked from the job: claimed, approved, paid. Warranty dates per serial number; a job on a unit under warranty opens the claim automatically.

Mangrove Marine

Berths and dry storage on a map, contracts per boat and owner, monthly billing with reminders, owners' requests in a daily list. The marina boat is the same record the workshop services.
